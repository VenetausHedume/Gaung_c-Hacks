Phase 2 · Pharmacy Stock & Fair-Billing Verification Portal

Verify stock & fair billing, earn community trust.

Pharmacies submit Stock Shelf Photographs and Sample GST Billing Invoices. MediCheck automatically cross-checks the billed medicine against NPPA DPCO ceiling rates (+5% GST) and CDSCO NSQ batch recalls. Stores that pass all criteria earn the Verified Pharmacy Badge and are suggested to patients searching their area.

Criterion 1 (25 pts): Stock Shelf Proof + ≥10 essential medicines Criterion 2 (30 pts): GST Invoice Proof submitted for audit Criterion 3 (25 pts): Billed unit rate ≤ NPPA Ceiling + 5% GST Criterion 4 (10+10 pts): Clean CDSCO Batch + Jan Aushadhi counter
