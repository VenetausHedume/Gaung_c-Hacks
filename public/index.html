Section 2 · The Problem

Three systemic failures at the pharmacy counter.

Patients face inflated MRPs, missing essential generics, and batch-level quality failures — while regulatory recovery takes decades and never refunds the buyer.

Failure 01 · Price & Trade Margins 100%–300% Margin

Inflated MRPs & ₹2,700 → ₹27,000 Markups

In September 2026, the Supreme Court questioned how a cancer medicine bought by retailers for ₹2,700 could carry a printed MRP of ₹27,000. Wholesale price audits revealed retail profits of 100% to 300% even on reputed brands, because there is no nationwide rule capping retail margins on all drugs at 16%.

Proof It Is Fixable: A 2019 pilot on 42 anti-cancer drugs cut MRPs by up to 90% across 526 brands, and in Oct 2026 NPPA capped trade margins on 137 anti-cancer drugs at 30% (saving ₹2,500 Cr/yr).
Balanced Counter-View: Government filings state that only ~11% of the non-scheduled market has trade margins above 30%.
Failure 02 · Substandard Batches 3,012 NSQ / Yr

Failed Batches on Retail Shelves

In FY 2025–26, CDSCO tested 1.41 lakh drug samples and flagged 3,012 Not-of-Standard-Quality (NSQ) and 283 spurious/adulterated samples. In Jan–Mar 2026 alone, 576 samples failed; March 2026 had 168 failures, and September 2026 flagged 330 NSQ + 1 spurious batch.

Why Batch-Level Checking Matters: CDSCO explicitly notes that NSQ findings apply to specific batch numbers (e.g. dissolution or assay failure), not entire brands — yet patients have no way to check their strip’s batch number at purchase.
Failure 03 · Stockouts & Enforcement Only 58 Complaints

Missing Generics & 20-Year Litigation

Field checks across Haryana, Ludhiana Civil Hospital, Delhi (Lok Nayak), Handwara (J&K), and Bengaluru documented routine stockouts at Jan Aushadhi & AMRIT counters due to monthly supply cycles. Meanwhile, the official portal Pharma Jan Samadhan recorded only 58 complaints in 2017–18 (42 on shortages).

Parliamentary Committee Finding (Aug 2026): 85% of NPPA’s ₹10,029.8 Cr overcharging demand is unrecovered (~70% stuck in court, some >20 years), and recovered funds are never returned to the overcharged patient.
Section 2.4 · Why Existing Tools Do Not Close The Gap

One place for price, quality & proof.

Pharma Sahi Daam checks ceiling prices but doesn’t screen batches or log complaints. CDSCO posts monthly PDFs nobody reads at the counter. E-pharmacies are marketplaces, not accountability tools.
